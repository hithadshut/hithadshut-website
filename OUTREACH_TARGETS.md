## Cadence

- **Weeks 1-12 (months 1-3):** Tier 1 only. 5 pitches per week, max.
  Quality > volume. Each pitch personalized.
- **Months 4-6:** Tier 2 + 3. 8-10 pitches per week. Begin to see
  rhythm — what hooks land vs what gets ignored.
- **Months 7-12:** Tier 4-5. By now Ofek should have first 5-10 backlinks
  from earlier tiers; cite those as social proof in new pitches.

## Realistic conversion rates

- Cold pitch → reply: **15-25%** if highly personalized.
- Reply → link/citation live: **30-50%** if the angle is solid.
- Net: ~5-10% of cold pitches turn into a published mention.

50 cold pitches → 2-5 backlinks. Plan accordingly.
